Where it comes from

Inspired by Photon. Not Photon.

Cornea chases the warm, soft look of Photon by SixthSurge. Here is how close the two really are, and where the line sits.

A

Photon was the target

The goal was Photon's mood at a higher frame rate on a modest card. The comparison screenshots on the Features page are Photon, shown only so you can judge for yourself.

GOAL
B

Its source was read as a reference

The AI studied Photon's shader files to learn techniques such as the sky look-up table, the light volume for coloured torches, soft shadows and temporal anti-aliasing. Photon's licence allows examining and learning from its source.

STUDY
C

The code was written fresh

Cornea's files were written new, with their own structure and many different choices, and no Photon file was copied in. But reading Photon shaped the design, so the two share ideas and some layout. We can't promise no passage ever resembles it.
